# Proposed bio changes (waiting for Param's OK, not written)

Each change only removes a claim no source backs, fixes a spelling, or updates a fact a source contradicts. Nothing new is invented.

1. midnight-traffic. Sources (Four/Four 2023, Wild City 2023 and 2025) describe one producer, Rishi, not a duo. Boiler Room session page (31 May 2022) lists Nikki Nair, Parris, Midnight Traffic and SUCHI (not Murthovic).
   New: Midnight Traffic is Rishi, a Hyderabad-based techno producer and DJ on Qilla Records. He played Boiler Room Hyderabad in May 2022 with Nikki Nair, Parris and SUCHI. His track Mantis is on Qilla's Chakravyuh compilation (2024). In April 2026 he played DOMMUNE's BROAD.J series in Japan.

2. murthovic. The Boiler Room Hyderabad 2022 page does not list him. Also drops "pioneering" and "cutting-edge", and the Melbourne line written as a future date.
   New: Murthovic (MSR Murthy) is a Hyderabad DJ, producer, sound practitioner and researcher with close to 20 years in electronic music, and a Magnetic Fields regular. With visual artist Thiruda (Avinash Kumar) he created Elsewhere In India, a transmedia project that joins game engine technology with live performance, set in 2079 with digital avatars of both artists. Their project Jal, Jungle, Zameen debuted at Magnetic Fields Nomads in Khetri, Rajasthan in February 2026: a 60-minute live electronica set with hereditary musicians Gopika, Sawai, Jakir, Dilip and Roshan on kamaycha, morchang, bhapang, khartal and dholak. He has also played audiovisual Bharatnatyam DJ sets in Hyderabad (2023).

3. discokid. Mixmag (The Lab Goa, 2022) says Paradise Disco is her property; it does not call her its resident DJ.
   New: Discokid is the DJ name of Fatema Hakim, a disco and funk DJ from India. Her property Paradise Disco hosted the first Disco Diwali in 2021. She played Mixmag's The Lab Goa in January 2022.

4. chrms. No source mentions Krunk. Wild City, Rolling Stone India and RA name him as Veer Kowli from Mumbai.
   New: chrms is Veer Kowli, a Mumbai producer making future bass and electro. His releases include Lover Boy (2019) and PILOT on Hypercolour Records (2024).

5. prismer. No public source for the alias Don Jiro. Also drops "one of India's brightest artists", "prestigious" and "historic".
   New: Prismer is Rohan Kale, a New Delhi producer and DJ making explorative, bass-driven electronic music that runs from techno and jungle to house, dubstep, breakbeat and disco. He first got into electronic music in London in 2010. His early releases came out on Boxout.FM, Reel Long Overdub and Tokyo Hell. He has shared stages with DJ Stingray, Pangaea, Denham Audio, Ivy Lab and Rider Shafique, and played Magnetic Fields, Bass Camp, Grasslands (Nepal) and Boiler Room Delhi NCR (June 2024). He played The Warehouse Project India in October 2025; his Mumbai set came out on Apple Music. He started the Delhi party series Mixed Feelings, made as a safe space for marginalised communities.

6. dreamstates. DGTL India and Mixmag Asia describe a Mumbai-based DJ with no releases found, not a producer.
   New: dreamstates is a Mumbai-based DJ playing house, techno, breakbeat, acid and electro. He hosts the monthly Boxout.fm show Cosmic Claps and has played DGTL India 2025, Echoes of Earth and Magnetic Fields.

7. green-park. Spelling per Bandcamp and Rolling Stone India: Sidharth Gupta, Rishabh Bhan Singh.

8. okedo. Spelling per Wild City 2020: Will Clarke.

9. dualist-inquiry. Album spelling: Doppelganger (MusicBrainz and reviews; our release row already has it right).

10. kiss-nuka. "Mumbai-based" becomes "based between India and Spain" (her agency, One House).

11. hunee. "now based in Amsterdam" becomes "now based in Berlin" (his Bandcamp shows Berlin, Germany).
