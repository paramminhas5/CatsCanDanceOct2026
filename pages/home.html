House rules

We've spent years on dance floors in this country. This is what we wished we'd had on the way there, at the door, and on the drive home.

  1. Picked by hand.If we wouldn't go ourselves, it isn't listed.
  2. Electronic music, taken seriously.No Bollywood nights. No filler.
  3. Everything has a source.Every record and gig on an artist's page says where it came from.
  4. You learn by playing.School is an instrument, not a video course.
